## Running it in the integration add-on

In the add-on you downloaded with "Download integration test add-on" at the top right, use the stick to pick the example in the picker, or run the command. In-game text is in English: the picker (`json-ui-react Examples`) lists this example as `Research screen`. Until you pick an item, the detail area shows `Pick a research.`.

2022 buttons, form title `jur:re`.

```text
/scriptevent jur:open research
```
